I was most impressed by **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** out of the three cloud platforms due to its robust AI, ML, and Kubernetes capabilities. Additionally, I appreciated that Google Cloud offers services that can facilitate large-scale data processing and contemporary applications. 

Because they all offer fundamental cloud services like computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and identity management, AWS, Azure, and GCP share many characteristics. Additionally, all three offer scalable resources, global infrastructure, and management consoles that let users design and oversee cloud services.

Because every organization has different objectives, budgets, technologies, and workloads, business requirements play a significant role in cloud platform selection. For instance, a business that already uses Microsoft products might gain more from Azure, an AI-focused business might favor GCP, and an enterprise that requires a wide range of services might select AWS.

I learned that selecting a cloud provider involves more than just comparing well-known platforms while working as a Cloud Solutions Consultant. Before suggesting a solution, I learned to take into account the client's particular needs, scalability, performance, current technology, availability, and possible use cases.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio became more structured and educational after completing this task.
